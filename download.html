Download XGP

Testnet preview · Windows 10 and 11

The public launch is coming soon. Right now the client is for testers: it runs on the XRP Ledger testnet, and items and dollars in it have no real value.

Download for Windows (testers)

The client keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, it downloads in the background and asks you to restart.

Installing

  1. Run XGP-Setup.exe. It installs for your Windows account only and doesn't need admin rights.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the preview isn't code-signed yet.
  3. Open XGP from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut, and sign in with Xaman.

Uninstalling

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → XGP → Uninstall. Your items stay in your wallet; they live on the ledger, not in the app.
