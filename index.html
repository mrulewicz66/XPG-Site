How it works

XGP is a desktop client and store, like the game launchers you know, with one difference: what you earn belongs to you.

Sign in with Xaman

No passwords or emails. You approve sign-in in the Xaman wallet on your phone, and your keys never leave it.

Earn items as you play

Games award items as NFTs on the XRP Ledger. You claim them into your own wallet with one approval.

Trade for dollars

List items on the market in US dollars (RLUSD). A sale pays the seller, the game's developer and the platform in one ledger transaction.

Friends and community

Profiles, friends, chat and a feed of what your friends earn, buy and play.
